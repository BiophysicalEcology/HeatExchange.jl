API Reference

⚖️ Heat balance

Solar and longwave radiation, convection, conduction, evaporation, respiration and metabolism, summed into a budget whose residual is zero at steady state.

🌡️ Temperature or metabolic rate

Find the body temperature for a given metabolism, or the metabolic rate that holds a given core temperature, with bare skin or with insulation.

🧥 Fur, feathers and fat

Heat conducted from the core through flesh, fat and a porous coat as a stack of radial layers, with radiation and conduction through the fibres.

🐕 Bodies of many parts

A back and a belly, or a trunk, head and limbs, each with its own heat budget, sharing a core or conducting heat to each other.

🍃 Leaves

The same heat budget with stomatal conductance in place of wet skin, for leaf temperature and transpiration.

🦎 For NicheMapR users

How the ectotherm and endotherm models of NicheMapR map onto this package, and what has changed.

📐 Differentiable

Written so that an optimiser can drive the heat budget with automatic differentiation, as BiophysicalBehaviour.jl does.

📏 Units

Every temperature, heat flow and trait is a Unitful.jl quantity.

Manual ​

HeatExchange.jl computes the exchange of heat, and of the water that goes with it, between an organism and its environment. Given the organism's size, shape, surface properties and physiology, and the air temperature, wind, humidity and radiation around it, it solves the steady-state heat budget for the body temperature, or for the metabolic rate needed to hold a body temperature.

It is part of the BiophysicalEcology ecosystem for mechanistic niche modelling. The bodies come from BiophysicalGeometry.jl, the environments from Microclimate.jl, and the behaviour that changes both from BiophysicalBehaviour.jl. See the Introduction for the design of the package, Environments and the ecosystem for how the packages fit together, and For NicheMapR users for its origin in NicheMapR.
